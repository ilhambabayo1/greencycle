About GreenCycle

GreenCycle helps you track everything you recycle — plastic, paper, glass, metal, e-waste and organic waste — and rewards you with points for every kilogram you keep out of landfill.

  • ♻️ Log recycled items with title, category and weight
  • 🏆 Earn points automatically based on category and weight
  • 📊 See lifetime totals of items, weight and points
  • 🔍 Filter items by category
